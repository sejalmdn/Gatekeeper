- Required references to earlier turns

- Allowed/Forbidden tool not selected
- Arguments are valid JSON, required, forbidden, exact, and range-based argument checks
- Values correctly copied from scenario input/history
- Across attempts: tool-selection rate, argument-validity rate, and selection stability
